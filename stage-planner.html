What your computer needs

The planner builds the school hall in 3D inside your browser, as you work: the stage and its sets, the LED wall, every spotlight and hundreds of audience seats. It is made for a computer like this.

Computer
A laptop or desktop. Phones and tablets can open it, but the panels and controls are made for a larger screen, a mouse and a keyboard.
Browser
A recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, with hardware acceleration on. The planner draws with WebGL 2 and cannot start without it.
Graphics
Built-in graphics are enough for the hall in fast mode. Balanced and best are for a computer with a separate graphics card.
Memory
8 GB or more. With the hall open, the browser uses about 0.5 to 0.75 GB, and saving a 4K picture can take it towards 2 GB.
Screen
1280 × 720 or larger. Where the top bar has no room for them, the camera, snapping and quality menus move into its File menu.
Controls
A mouse or trackpad, and a keyboard. Drag to move a part, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom; R rotates, Ctrl + Z undoes.
Walk through
The mouse to look around, the keyboard to walk. Click the hall, then W A S D or the arrow keys; Esc frees the mouse. Touch screens get arrows on screen.
Snapshot
Pictures up to 3840 × 2160 (4K), with the best shadows in any mode. The first picture takes longest, while those shadows are prepared: on built-in graphics, allow up to a minute.

Quality

  • FastEvery spotlight still lights the hall, but none casts a shadow; no glow or smoothing.Opens in this
  • BalancedShadows from the 4 brightest spotlights, glow and smoothing; sharper on high-resolution screens.
  • BestShadows from the 8 brightest spotlights, and sharper still.

Change it at any time from the quality menu, in the top bar or, on a narrower window, in its File menu.
