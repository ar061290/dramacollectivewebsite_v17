Epilogue

The following day, Luis visits the nearby community college; he is immediately admitted in less than an hour and is in class!

Luis gradually adapts to this new way of living; he learns new things every day and has no regrets about his choice. The second 40-year-old in their family who is enrolled in school makes Ellery and Anna happy. In the end, everyone is content.

After all,
Sometimes change is beautiful.
