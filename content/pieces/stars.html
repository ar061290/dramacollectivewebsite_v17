Stars.
When you look up at the night sky, the first thing you see in the ever expanding night are the tiny specks of light.
Stars, the magic of the past swirling into life.
Stars, so far away glinting mischievously in the dark.
Stars, so ever present in our chaotic world.
Stars, the one grounded thing that we all observe.
Stars, no matter where you are, are the one thing that will remain constant.
Stars.
