Become a sponsor

Partner with the next generation of theatre

Your brand. Our stage. A shared journey.

Corporate sponsorship today is increasingly about more than visibility. It is about creating meaningful associations with causes that make a difference.

Your sponsorship will help create an environment where young people can build these skills, express themselves freely and develop their potential through theatre. By supporting this initiative, your organisation can become a meaningful enabler of youth development and the performing arts.

Sponsorship opportunities

Sponsorship tiers

  • Visibility & recognition

    • Brand logo on event posters
    • Brand mention during the performance
    • Sponsor name included in the event programme

  • Enhanced visibility & engagement

    • Brand logo on event posters and banners
    • Social media promotion
    • Brand mention during the performance
    • Brand logo or name in the event programme

  • Premium brand association

    • Prominent brand logo placement on event posters and banners
    • Multiple social media promotions
    • Special mention during the performance
    • Prominent brand logo or name in the publication, production, and outreach
    • Special acknowledgment on stage

All tiers are negotiable.

What your support will enable

Sponsorship contributions directly support the production and the young performers by helping fund:

  • Costumes and accessories
  • Props and stage requirements
  • Set design and set pieces
  • Technical and production equipment
  • Promotional and audience outreach activities
  • Other essential production requirements

The impact of your support

Every contribution helps transform an idea on paper into a performance on stage.

The real return on your sponsorship is not measured only in impressions or logo placements. It is measured in the confidence of a young performer walking onto a stage, the courage to express an idea, the ability to work as part of a team and the belief that their creativity has value.

Because every great performance begins with someone believing in the performer.
