About us

Where young talent finds its voice

Theatre is more than performance, it is confidence. It is imagination. It is self-discovery.

Who we are

The CHIREC Drama Collective is a group of young performers from the #1 international school in Telangana who come together to learn, experiment, create and put on a show.

Skills that stay after the curtain closes

These students are not only putting on a show for you, they are building skills that will stay with them long after the curtain closes.

  • Communication
  • Collaboration
  • Discipline
  • Empathy
  • Leadership
  • Creative problem solving

For a young performer, stepping on a stage is an act of courage.

It takes confidence to face an audience, discipline to rehearse, teamwork to create a production and creativity to turn a script into a living experience.

What we stand for

  • Youth development and nurturing talent
  • Creativity and innovation
  • Education beyond the classroom
  • Teamwork and leadership
  • Arts and cultural development
  • Community engagement

We are building a sustainable platform where young performers can learn, collaborate and experiment through productions, workshops and future theatre initiatives.
