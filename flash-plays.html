What a flash play is

A flash play is a short, focused piece of theatre made around a particular event, theme, idea or collaboration. It could be a scripted scene, an adaptation or a performance the students create themselves, and it is developed and performed in a relatively short time.

The CHIREC Drama Collective aims, through regular flash plays, to create opportunities for students to perform beyond the annual school production, while furthering CHIREC’s image as a school that fosters creative expression.

The idea is to make theatre more immediate, accessible and responsive to what is happening across the school, and to give students the chance to write, direct, perform and experiment with different forms of storytelling.
