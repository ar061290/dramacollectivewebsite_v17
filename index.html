What we do

Five ways onto the stage

  1. Production

    Once a year, a full-scale play — written, directed, designed, performed and managed entirely by students.

    The production

  2. Flash plays

    Short plays that take on the real issues students deal with: academic and peer pressure, cyberbullying and safeguarding.

    Flash plays

  3. Workshops

    Bi-weekly, senior-led. Improvisation, method acting, miming and staging, alongside theatre history.

    Workshops

  4. Publication

    Our own independent title: theatre history, reflections, interviews, and original student work.

    The publication

  5. Outreach

    Mime and mute theatre with Project SignVerse, and collaborations with Chirec Radio.

    Social outreach
