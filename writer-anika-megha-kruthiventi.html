About Anika Megha Kruthiventi

Hi, I’m Anika. I’m curious about almost everything, which is probably why I’m always doing three things at once. I love science, especially the little “why” behind things, but I also love writing, creating and turning random ideas into actual projects. I’m naturally quite curious and probably ask too many questions, but I think that’s how I learn best. I’m still figuring out where all these interests will take me, but I’m having fun finding out.
