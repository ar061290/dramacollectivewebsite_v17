Beauty in Imperfection

By Mehek Jain

Beauty in imperfection, a paradox untold,
A secret hidden in plain sight, waiting to unfold.
It’s in the cracks of a broken vase,
That light shines through and its beauty erases.
It’s in the laughter lines around eyes,
That speaks of a life lived, with joy and surprise,
And the scars that adorn our skin,
Reminding us of battles won and battles within.
It’s in the unevenness of a handmade quilt,
That weaves a story of love, time and effort felt,
And in the crooked smile of a child,
That brings joy to all who sees its beaming wild.
We search for perfection in a world that’s flawed,
In a world that’s alive, with chaos and applause,
But beauty lies in the imperfections we see,
In the uniqueness that sets us all free.
For life is not about a flawless exterior,
But the inner journey, a road that’s much clearer,
And the path we take to reach our soul,
Is the journey where beauty in imperfection takes its toll.
So let’s embrace our quirks, our flaws and our scars,
For they are the gifts, the memories and the stars,
That light up our lives, guiding us to our true north,
Where beauty in imperfection blooms with worth.
